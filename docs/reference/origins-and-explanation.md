# Origins and ultimate explanation reference collection

Reviewed October 13, 2026. The source-backed entries in `lib/prism-reference.js`
are owner-authorized reference material, not fixed responses or additions to
the authoritative corpus. They cover common ancestry, ancient floods,
irreducible complexity, abiogenesis (including attributed Tour critiques),
Darwin and eyes, noncoding DNA and prediction quality, and PSR/uncertainty.

The existing `getCuratedLearningContext` wrapper attaches only matching entries
to initial and follow-up retrieval. At most four entries are selected; explicit
topic matches precede broad creationism orientation. No extra AI call, database
migration, query logging, or user-facing automatic answer is introduced. Local
references remain available if approved-learning database retrieval fails.

Notes separate evidence from interpretation and limitations, identify source
types, and carry review dates. Linked sources are pointers, not live retrieval
or verbatim excerpts. Update claims against newer findings where needed.
Historical sources remain evidence of what their authors claimed; they are
not automatically evidence that those claims are correct.

Apply consistent scrutiny rather than requiring equal support. A successful
prediction need not be unique; discrimination matters for favoring one model.
PSR is a philosophical argument, not simply a gap in experimental knowledge.
"IDK of the gaps" denotes asymmetric dismissal from ignorance, not honest
suspension of judgment. Rejecting a deficient theistic inference does not prove
God's absence, and an unknown does not by itself prove God's involvement.

Validation: `node tests/test-prism-reference.mjs` checks relevant/unrelated
routing, bounded selection, source metadata, and database failure behavior.
