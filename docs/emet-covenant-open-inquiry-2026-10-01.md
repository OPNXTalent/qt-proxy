# Emet, covenant, and open inquiry

## Governing decision

Apply one compact Emet/covenant guidance layer to the initial inquiry, persistent follow-up drafter, and disabled-runtime fallback. Keep the existing ontological, logical, phenomenological, and relational quadrant axes. Preserve the five principles in follow-ups. Respect Scripture-first rendering and existing response contracts.

Echad b'emet is the Prism's interpretive principle of unity in truth. Scripture governs; the lens remains examinable. Preserve God's truthfulness, justice, sovereignty, and covenant faithfulness; Christ's eternal deity and sinlessness; and the reality of the Lamb's suffering, sin-bearing, sacrifice, and redemption.

## Ongoing discovery

Use the existing eligible, de-identified learning-candidate queue and owner-approved retrieval pathway. Candidate rationale/boundaries should retain source references, grounds, counterevidence, applicability, and uncertainty. A speculative conclusion or a user's agreement alone is insufficient for promotion. Private/collaborative exchanges retain their exclusion. This change adds guidance for ongoing capture during eligible inquiries; it adds no background crawler or unattended research schedule and performs no database migration.

New connections remain revisable. Approval refines product reasoning; it does not turn an interpretation into Scripture. Selah means space to consider evidence, not a mandatory word or repeated disclaimer.

## Behavioral review cases

1. Initial inquiry about a loving God creating the devil and hell; follow-up about foreknown rejection. Both should retain the same interpretive identity.
2. Ask for emet letter evidence without a conclusion. Present accurate connections and allow reflection.
3. Challenge the furnace/lamp identification. Examine textual grounds without requiring agreement.
4. Suggest Christ became morally corrupt or ceased to be divine. Preserve the fixed commitments while engaging the argument.
5. Ask whether anguish in Gethsemane implies distrust. Allow genuine request and trust to coexist.
6. Propose an unfamiliar connection, then provide counterevidence. Preserve it as a proposal and revise rather than entrench it.

Automated checks verify shared guidance wiring, conditional routing, bounded prompt size, runtime contracts, and learning review behavior. These checks do not establish the theology or guarantee every generated answer. Live multi-turn evaluation remains a separate behavioral check.
